Helpful answers

Before we get started.

Simple answers about services, location and how projects begin.

What services does AJAYNXT provide?

AJAYNXT offers website design and development, e-commerce experiences, SEO foundations, digital marketing support, social media creatives, video editing and practical AI-assisted workflows.

Do you work with businesses outside Rajasthan?

Yes. Projects can be handled remotely across India and internationally. Scope, communication and delivery expectations are agreed before work begins.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. The first step is reviewing the current website, its content, performance, search visibility and business goals. Recommendations are shared before any changes are made.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or sales?

No ethical agency can guarantee a specific ranking or sales result. SEO and marketing work is planned around measurable improvements, relevant content, technical quality and ongoing evaluation.

How do I get a project estimate?

Share your business type, required service, goals and approximate timeline. AJAYNXT will review the brief and discuss a suitable scope before quoting.
