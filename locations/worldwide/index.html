Frequently asked questions

Can AJAYNXT work with my business remotely?

Yes. Projects can be coordinated remotely. Scope, communication, review points and delivery expectations are agreed before work begins.

Can you guarantee search rankings?

No specific ranking can be guaranteed. SEO work focuses on technical quality, useful content, search intent and ongoing measurement.

How do I request a quote?

Send your business details, the service you need and your goals through WhatsApp. AJAYNXT will review the brief and discuss the next step.

What to plan before your project

For international projects, agree on currency, ownership of design assets and hosting accounts, communication windows, and any localisation or accessibility requirements before work starts. Delivery depends on the agreed scope and review process.
