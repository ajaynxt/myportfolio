Google Ads audit checklist for lead generation.

Before increasing ad spend, check whether your search campaigns reach relevant customers and record meaningful enquiries.

1. Verify conversion tracking

Define a qualified lead, test the enquiry form and check whether the conversion records once. Separate primary bidding conversions from secondary actions. Where available, compare ad conversions with qualified CRM leads.

2. Review search terms and negative keywords

Classify visible queries by purchase intent, research intent and irrelevance. Investigate costly irrelevant searches and add negative keywords carefully so you do not accidentally block valuable demand.

3. Review geographic and language targeting

Check where leads actually come from against your service area. For international campaigns, verify country targeting, language, currency and relevant location settings. Avoid paying for enquiries your business cannot serve.

4. Match ads with landing pages

Check headline relevance, mobile speed, contact options and clear service details. Make sure the form or WhatsApp link works and that users understand the next step.

5. Review spend and lead quality

Compare cost per qualified lead and conversion rate by campaign. Low-cost clicks alone do not prove business value. Agree a test budget and evaluate sufficient data before drawing conclusions.

6. Prioritise the changes

Fix measurement first, then irrelevant traffic and landing page problems. Document each change and compare results over suitable windows. Avoid changing every setting at once without a clear hypothesis.
