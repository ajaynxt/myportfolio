SEO keyword research and search-intent mapping.

Choose search topics that match what potential customers need, then map them to useful pages rather than stuffing keywords into every section.

Understand customer searches

Start with your products, services, target audience, geographic coverage and customer questions. Separate informational searches from commercial and transactional intent so each page serves a clear purpose.

Competitor research and prioritisation

Review relevant competitors and search results to understand page formats, content gaps and service differentiation. Where available, use Keyword Planner, Search Console or other agreed tools to assess demand and competition. Estimates are not guaranteed search volumes.

Local and long-tail keyword mapping

Group related queries and map them to existing or planned service pages. Location targeting must reflect genuine service areas. Specific phrases such as business website pricing or restaurant website design can be addressed through helpful pages when relevant.

On-page SEO and progress checks

Use descriptive titles, headings, readable copy, useful internal links and appropriate image descriptions. Track indexability, impressions, relevant queries and enquiries using available tools. Google does not guarantee rankings, and no hidden keyword stuffing is used.

A keyword research brief can separate US, UK, Canadian, Australian, New Zealand and UAE search demand instead of treating worldwide searches as one market. Compare local terminology, service modifiers, competitor pages and commercial intent. Deliverables can include a prioritised keyword-to-page map, content gaps and PPC negative-keyword suggestions. Search volume and difficulty estimates depend on the tools and market data available.
