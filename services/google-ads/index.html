Campaign planning and keyword targeting

Search campaigns can focus on relevant, purchase-intent searches. We review service areas, search terms, keyword match types, negative keywords and ad copy before agreeing on the campaign structure.

Campaign formats and landing pages

Depending on the business and eligibility, we can plan Search, Display, YouTube, Shopping or Performance Max campaigns. E-commerce campaigns may require product feeds and Merchant Center setup. Landing pages should match the ad offer, work well on mobile and give customers a clear next step.

Conversion tracking and ongoing review

Agree on useful conversion actions such as enquiry submissions, calls or purchases. Review search terms, click costs, conversion rates and cost per lead using available account data. Tracking and consent configuration must match the website's actual setup.

Budget, access and campaign expectations

Advertising spend is separate from service fees. Your account and payment method remain under your control; changes and access are agreed before work. No fixed lead volume, cost per lead or return on ad spend is guaranteed.
