Pinterest profile and board strategy

Plan boards that reflect your real products, services and customer interests. Use descriptive board names, relevant pin titles and helpful descriptions rather than repeating unrelated keywords.

Pin creative and Pinterest SEO

Design vertical pins with clear imagery, legible text and a useful destination. Build themes around search intent, such as product collections, wedding inspiration, home design or travel planning where relevant to the business.

Pinterest Ads and measurement

Explore paid campaigns if advertising is available for the business's account, market and billing setup. Review campaign objectives, creative, destination pages and Pinterest Tag requirements where appropriate. Paid budgets and access are agreed separately.

Website traffic and reporting

Use relevant destination pages and consistent campaign tracking. Review outbound clicks, saves, impressions and attributable actions; agree a publishing cadence and test creative formats. Results vary by niche, demand and content quality.
